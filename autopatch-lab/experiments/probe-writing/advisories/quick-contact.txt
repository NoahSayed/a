Title: Quick Contact <= 1.0.0 - Missing Authorization to Unauthenticated Settings Update
CVE: CVE-2026-90001
CVSS: 5.3 (Medium) CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:L/A:N
CWE: Missing Authorization
Affected: quick-contact <= 1.0.0
Patched versions: 1.0.1

Description:
The Quick Contact plugin is vulnerable to unauthorized modification of data due to a missing capability check on the qc_save_settings AJAX action in versions up to, and including, 1.0.0. This makes it possible for unauthenticated attackers to change the address that contact form messages are sent to.
