# Verification: market / community / big-tech / China-vendor claims (checked 19 Nov 2026)

## Summary

- Verified with a skeptical eye, mostly via WebSearch result summaries. Direct fetches worked only for microsoft.com. github.blog, govinfosecurity.com, prnewswire.com, ona.com and churnmeon.vercel.app all failed with ENOTFOUND (host not in the session allowlist). I substituted other sources where I could and say so below. GitHub repos could not be inspected through `gh`: access is not enabled for this session.
- Of the 25 claims checked, **no fabrication was found in the main claims**. Most are confirmed. Corrections:
  1. Snyk's "85% fix accuracy" is an older Agent Fix figure. The Oct 2026 Evo release itself claims **90% mergeable-fix rate** (vendor claim).
  2. Aikido free KEV backports: the commitment is real. It is a press-release pledge. The notes' wording implies it is already a running free program, and no source shows that.
  3. The Linux kernel "432 CVEs in two days" is confirmed (19-20 Jul 2026). The notes' framing of Kroah-Hartman warning that LLM-found issues are still rising is **only partly supported**: The Register reports he attributed the spike partly to a **backlog** (vacations, conferences) plus broad CVE assignment.
  4. "Claude Security never auto-applies": the "never" wording is not in any source. The documented design is that patches are proposed for human review and applied only with approval.
  5. The Wiz price: $32B headline. The notes' $29.5B figure comes from the 10-Q and is plausible. I could not check it.
  6. ARTEX vs Korean banks: the claim is **real and well covered**, but it is absent from the notes I was given. The scale figures conflict (7 banks; about 66-68k people).
- **Full-loop question: no.** I found no product or OSS project that is publicly documented doing the entire loop (monitor CVE feeds and security news, decide if my repo is affected, patch, test, open a PR). Parts exist, and the closest candidates are listed in section B. This matches the notes' conclusion, with one caveat: vendor marketing for Ona, Seal and PR Churn implies "CVE published, then patch and PR". I could not verify any of it. Treat the gap as **real but thinner than the notes imply**.
- **Things to flag in the notes:**
  - "PR Churn" (churnmeon.vercel.app) is a single-source hit with no independent trace. Do not cite it.
  - The oometa.ai "CISA BOD 26-04 / KEV agent-exploited CVEs" item stays unverified. It is a single blog.
  - The Retrospective: Renovate 3.7.0 at Meta dev.to post looks fabricated. The notes already caveat it. I agree.
  - Whether the CyberGym ranks are real is unverifiable (see section D).

## E. Implications for "is there a market gap"

- Nothing found contradicts the notes' gap: no verified product combines news and exploit-chatter ingestion, a repo-affected check, a test-gated patch and a PR.
- The gap is narrowing from three sides: GitHub (agent on Dependabot alerts, GA agentic autofix), Snyk Evo (open preview), and Aikido+Root (backports). All three are triggered by their own vulnerability DB or scanner. All three keep a human reviewer.
- Platform cost and plan requirements are real barriers for small teams: GitHub needs Code Security plus Copilot plus credits, Snyk Evo is by account rep, and Claude Security is Enterprise-only. That, not product absence, is where a cheap indie tool has room.
- Evidence the human gate is the norm: GitHub, Snyk, Claude Security, CodeMender and Seal all stop at a draft PR or approval queue. The only no-human merging with real scale is deterministic maintenance PRs (Spotify).
- Caution on demand signals: Microsoft's 550k and Amazon's 4,500 developer-years are not evidence that AI patching works. Neither is documented as AI-written CVE fixes.
